# Portfolio item 1b: LPG Dependence in Nepali Households
 
A data analysis report examining household cooking fuel use across Nepal using the 2021 National Population and Housing Census, with a focus on LPG dependence and its implications for energy security.

## Research Topic
 
Over the past decade, Nepal has shifted away from traditional cooking fuels toward LPG — but Nepal produces no LPG domestically and relies entirely on imports. Recent geopolitical tensions affecting the Strait of Hormuz have contributed to shortages and price hikes felt directly by Nepali households. This report asks: **where is LPG dependence most concentrated, and which regions are most exposed when imports are disrupted?**
 
The analysis shows that LPG adoption is heavily urban, meaning the same cities that benefit most from cleaner cooking are also the most vulnerable to supply shocks. Rural areas remain dependent on wood, which insulates them from import disruptions but locks households into polluting fuels.
